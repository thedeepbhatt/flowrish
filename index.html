Questions

Straight answers.

Can you read PDFs, invoices and purchase orders?

Yes. We pull out the key details, such as dates, amounts, vendors and line items, into structured data. Anything unclear is flagged for a quick human check instead of being guessed.

Do I have to move to the cloud?

No. Seed runs on a single laptop and Grow on your own server. Cloud is an option for when you outgrow that.

How is this different from ChatGPT or Power BI?

Those work best once your data is already clean. We prepare and automate the data, run it where you want, and keep pricing flat, with a team that explains the numbers in plain language.

Who can see my data?

Only the people you give access to. It stays in your own laptop, server or cloud account, and we don’t keep a copy.

What if I stop working with you?

The system and your data are yours. Nothing is locked inside our platform.

How long does a first dashboard take?

A pilot on one real process, such as monthly sales or expenses, is typically about two weeks.
